## About Me

I'm pursuing an **M.S. in Financial Engineering** at **Columbia University** (expected December 2027).

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and operations research. I’m particularly interested in how predictions can inform decisions under uncertainty. On the machine learning side, I’m interested in building models for time-series data and understanding how well they generalize when conditions change. On the optimization and operations research side, I’m interested in formulating decision problems with practical constraints and exploring how stochastic and robust optimization can account for uncertainty.

I'm seeking internships in **quantitative finance, software engineering, and data science**.

## Projects

**[Futures Factor Weighting with LightGBM and XGBoost](https://github.com/xuanyu-song/futures-factor-weighting)**  
Compares tree-based factor weights, direct model predictions, and equal-weight baselines on held-out synthetic data.

**[Walk-Forward Factor Discovery with Symbolic Regression](https://github.com/xuanyu-song/walk-forward-factor-discovery)**  
Searches for factor expressions in rolling training windows and compares them with momentum and linear regression on held-out synthetic data.

**[Futures Backtesting Engine](https://github.com/xuanyu-song/futures-backtesting-engine)**  
Simulates next-open execution for futures signals and records positions, transaction costs, and equity.

**[Technical Indicator Robustness Across Futures Markets](https://github.com/xuanyu-song/futures-indicator-robustness)**  
Compares fixed, rolling, and cross-market MACD and RSI parameters on held-out synthetic data.
